# Consultant paid invoices — §10 design proposal

Status: root reviewed and approved implementation with the corrections below; no statutory approval or runtime verification claimed by this design. Scope: [Sonam #2458 §10](https://github.com/saeloun/miru-web/issues/2458), including split-credit completion. Source baseline: `cbb26ffae27225c9cbd2998beb9dc28ed6b68f11`.

## User flow and reviewed inputs

1. Add an Invoice panel to the payroll run and a private Invoices page in the consultant profile. Accounts sees preparation, credit readiness, document availability, current credit-review status and email outcome separately.
2. Before creation, explicitly choose **Generate in Miru** or **Link existing invoice**; neither is selected initially. Consultant or accounts can supply the existing original PDF and metadata; accounts reviews its match to the run. Record the declaration that the existing-invoice question was considered; Miru cannot discover undisclosed off-platform invoices.
3. Owner/admin can configure facts; freshly eligible assigned accounts or owner, different from the consultant, records the complete review. Required inputs default to **not reviewed**: billed entity name/address/country/tax identifiers or explicit not-applicable reason; document kind/treatment and required wording; invoice timing; issuer financial year/series/number; currency and any manually reviewed tax presentation; existing-invoice decision. Coding these configuration inputs needs no accountant interaction. Accounts destination is server-derived `accounts@saeloun.com`, displayed read-only, never a client/retry input.
4. Generate only where the recorded treatment supports the supplied facts and reconciles to approved payroll. No inferred GST/export exemption, statutory rate/calculation, tax-invoice deadline or legal certification. Unsupported treatment explicitly requires manual review and an existing externally prepared invoice; do not label that fallback all statutory automation.
5. Use explicitly reviewed/reserved FY/series/number, not a sequence engine; generated issue date is actual issuance day in Indian issuer `Asia/Kolkata`. Never accept a generated date from a request or backdate. Reconcile reviewed FY against actual date; a midnight change invalidates the staged candidate for one bounded rebuild, and changed FY requires fresh review. Validate issuer/FY/series/number uniqueness; conflicts require corrected review. No gapless or legally compliant numbering claim. If timing requires an earlier invoice, link it.
6. Ready preparation plus all confirmed credit automatically produces one paid invoice record for company/run/consultant and queues the PDF to the fixed trusted `accounts@saeloun.com`. Partial, submitted, processing or merely sent legs never qualify. Reviewed shortfall resolution retains its actual discrepancy; it is not an invented full bank receipt.
7. Existing mode preserves the original PDF/hash/number/date unchanged, marks the linked record Paid, and supplies a separate **Paid settlement statement** PDF for the accounts email and download. The statement references the original; it is not a replacement tax invoice. Generated mode creates the Paid invoice PDF itself. Spouse splits produce one consultant invoice with both effective transfers.
8. History and private downloads remain available to the kept owning identity after resignation, consent withdrawal or payment reopening. Show separate credit-under-review status after reopening; never rewrite/delete the invoice or its PDF. Corrections/credit notes remain explicit manual review, not silent regeneration. Discarded identities receive no access; do not extend general payroll-run visibility to consultants.

## Three records and immutable facts

- `PayrollInvoicePreparation`: append-only reviewed facts/command digest, previous receipt, actor/reviewer, company/run/employment, bound revision and billing receipt, mode, private original PDF descriptor; encrypted JSON and filtered serialization. Allow only operational claim token/state/start/error columns to change. A new review supersedes the prior receipt under run → employment locks; stale expected receipt/changed replay is rejected.
- `PayrollConsultantInvoice`: immutable encrypted snapshot, required preparation/revision/company/run/employment/issuer FKs, generated consent receipt FK, exactly one Mercury-group or India-handoff FK, immutable private PDF blob FKs and plaintext-byte SHA/length descriptors. Existing mode also requires the original blob. DB unique `(company_id, payroll_run_id, employment_id)` and `(company_id, issuer_user_id, financial_year, number_digest)`; number digest includes reviewed series and normalized number with a keyed digest. Issuer remains the consultant, not spouse/funding bank. No duplicate search across independent companies is promised.
- `PayrollConsultantInvoiceDelivery`: durable attempt number, queued/sending/sent/failed/uncertain state, claim token/timestamps, reviewed reconciliation and encrypted evidence. Unique invoice/attempt and partial unique active queued/sending/uncertain attempt. Initial attempt is inserted with the invoice; enqueue happens after commit.
- Migration: real FKs/restrict deletion, source XOR/state/positive-attempt checks, uniqueness above; composite invoice→preparation FK on `(preparation_id, company_id, payroll_run_id, employment_id)` with matching preparation unique key. Existing-parent FKs remain ordinary: tenant/run/subject equality is enforced by locked service checks and negative tests, not claimed as a composite DB guarantee. Reject invoice UPDATE/DELETE and reviewed-preparation-field UPDATE in DB triggers; operational fields remain writable.
- Freeze exact approved financial revision/line digest, historical billing submission/review and terms/service-period identity. Resolve and integrity-check that bound receipt rather than substitute today's profile. Old credited lines lacking a binding need an explicitly supplemental reviewed preparation, never a rewritten old approval/transfer digest.
- Snapshot/PDF includes both legal identities, number/date/period, separate consultancy fees, approved adjustments, itemized reimbursements, withholding, net entitlement, INR/USD settlement details/rate and each actual credit/date/reference. OUR is a separate company bank cost, excluded from consultant income and invoice totals. No payroll recalculation. Keep discrepant/over-credit facts visible.
- Snapshot/receipt facts use Rails encryption; PDFs use existing private ActiveStorage with authenticated streamed downloads and hash verification. Do not equate private storage with new application-level PDF encryption or invent a cryptographic framework.

## Issuance, recovery and access

Lock order for issue/review: run → Mercury group and instruction IDs ascending OR India handoff → employment → current preparation. No rendering, blob upload or mail inside those locks. All bank credit mutations retain their existing transactions.
Claim current preparation with a token, build the frozen candidate/number/date and stable source-credit digest, then render/stage outside locks. Reacquire locks, return an already issued invoice first, otherwise recheck current preparation/token, exact source/credit digest, approved revision/billing integrity, full-credit state and current reviewer authority. Generated mode must re-read `InvoiceAuthorization#receipt` with employment locked through INSERT; no consent-after-revision rule. Linking a pre-existing invoice is not on-behalf generation and records provenance without inventing a grant. A superseded claim/withdrawal/reopened credit loses cleanly; purge only its own unused staged blobs, never borrowed originals or a winner's blobs.

Use `PayrollPaymentGroup` and `PayrollIndiaHandoff` after-update-commit transitions to non-null `fully_credited_at`, plus preparation after-create-commit, to enqueue by run/employment. This covers Mercury, India and PTO reviewed prior-credit completion without editing the three financial setters. Enqueue failures log safe IDs/codes and cannot undo committed credit. Every five minutes recover at most 100 eligible oldest queued/waiting/stale preparations and queued deliveries, including pre-feature credited runs; rotate with `next_attempt_at` so blocked rows do not starve others. Recheck real full-credit predicates, not timestamp alone. Failed preparation retry is explicit; stale generation claims can retry because no document/email escaped and final uniqueness governs.

Delivery mirrors IndiaDelivery: one durable claim, attachment construction outside locks, final document/recipient/reviewer recheck immediately before transport. Known pre-transport failure is failed; exception after possible SMTP acceptance or interrupted sending is uncertain, never automatically resent. Accounts reconciles evidence, then explicitly retries the same immutable PDF if confirmed unsent. Mail failure never changes payment or invoice facts.

Own-history policy requires kept matching user/employment and current workspace, not active dates or continued payroll enablement; manager policy requires current eligible owner/assigned accounts. HTTP is authenticated, company scoped, private/no-store/nosniff; writes require CSRF and expected workspace/receipt. Reauthorize workspace/role/blob after reading before sending bytes. No public ActiveStorage URLs. Remittance-document receipt remains a separate existing/future §11 fact, not falsely completed here.
