# Employer deduction declarations and evidence

Bounded next delivery after salary slips: staff record INR deduction/exemption claims for FY 2025-26 or TY 2026-27, attach private PDF/JPEG/PNG evidence, submit, and receive an owner/admin review. Approval records a manually reviewed amount; it does not calculate eligibility, change salary or claim filing completion.

Use employment ownership and existing dashboard/API/UI/audit patterns. Every saved version is immutable except its workflow decision. A correction creates a linked draft, retaining earlier evidence and decisions; one child per version prevents competing revision branches. An approved predecessor remains effective while its revision is pending and is withdrawn atomically when the replacement is approved. Employees can withdraw their claims; reviewers cannot approve their own. Only the latest submitted version can be reviewed. Five files per version, 5 MiB each, actual type checks, private storage and authenticated downloads. No generic blob URLs/identifiers in responses. No general bookkeeper/manager access to coworkers' claims.

The category inventory uses familiar legacy labels for manual review, explicitly without verified current-year calculation support. Other claims need a description. Store decimal INR amounts, reject malformed or over-precision inputs, and cap approved amounts at the claim. Reviewer reasons are required for every decision. Keep financial/medical details out of audit payloads; audit only state changes and document access.

Change surface: migration/schema, declaration model and employment association, policy, API/routes, dashboard page/navigation/translations, focused request specs and product guide. Reimbursements continue through existing Expenses; payroll settlement controls, compensation/opening balances and statutory calculations remain subsequent work.
