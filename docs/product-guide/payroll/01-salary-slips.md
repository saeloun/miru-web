id: salary-slips
title: Salary slips
---

Open **Salary slips** in the sidebar to download documents published by your
employer. Staff can see only their own published slips in the current workspace.
Filter by pay month or use **All months** and the page controls to browse older
documents.

Owners and admins can import approved PDF slips from their payroll provider.
Select up to 20 PDFs at a time, each no larger than 5 MiB, enter the
provider/source, and assign every file to an employee and pay month. **Save
drafts** uploads the documents without making them visible to employees. Review
the file, employee and month before choosing **Publish salary slip**.

Publishing a correction replaces the employee's current slip for that month and
retains the earlier document as withdrawn history for owners/admins. **Withdraw
salary slip** removes employee access without deleting the original. Import a
new revision to correct a withdrawn slip. Downloads require authentication and
are not public sharing links. Employment/workspace deletion follows Miru's
existing deletion behavior and removes the associated salary-slip records and
attachments.

Each file is saved independently, so a failed upload does not stop later files.
Successful files leave the upload queue and appear as drafts. Failed files keep
their employee/month assignments and show a filename-specific error; correct or
remove them before retrying. You can remove a file and select it again.

Publication and withdrawal require confirmation. If the action is rejected, the
confirmation shows the error and remains open. **Cancel** closes it without
sending another action; starting a new confirmation clears the previous error.
Publication, withdrawal and downloads are recorded in the company audit log.

This archive stores approved provider documents. It does not calculate salary,
determine tax deductions, execute salary payments, or issue statutory TDS
certificates. Publishing a slip does not confirm payment. Use **Tax
declarations** for deduction claims and evidence review. India payroll
calculations, consultant withholding, GST and year-end tax preparation are
separate planned capabilities.
