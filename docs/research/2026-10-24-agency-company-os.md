# Miru: free, India-first Agency / Company OS

Research date: 24 October 2026. Recommendation: finish one Indian service-company operating cycle—people and documents → approved work and expenses → invoices/payables → reconciled monthly books → employer compliance handoff—before expanding into a general ERP or personal tax-filing product. The proposed implementation below is original; competitor sources establish useful capabilities, not designs or code to reproduce.

## Accounting invariants and migration

These are proposed design requirements, not a claim that the current models already implement an accounting ledger.

1. **Balanced and attributable:** every posted entry balances debit/credit in the legal entity's base currency; decimal/minor-unit money, currency, effective date, immutable approved source snapshot/version and posting actor are preserved. Tax, fees, receivables, payables and cash are separate accounts. Gross invoiced revenue and cash collected are different measures.
2. **Post once:** one stable source-event key creates one atomic posting. Provider callbacks, jobs and imports may retry. Matching an existing bank receipt to an invoice must not create a second receipt/revenue entry. Splits, partial payments, fees, transfers and unapplied amounts retain allocations.
3. **Correct visibly:** posted facts are corrected with linked reversals/adjustments and credit/debit documents; a paid/closed transaction cannot be silently edited or deleted. Server-side period locks apply across controllers, imports and jobs. Preserve audit/attachments independently of a user's access deactivation; current employment deletion cascades need review before claiming statutory retention.
4. **No invented FX:** preserve native/base currencies and amounts, actual conversion or valuation method, rate/date/source and realized FX on settlement. [Payment](../../app/models/payment.rb) can fall back to an invoice rate or 1:1; [Expense](../../app/models/expense.rb) also has a 1:1 fallback. Those operational fallbacks must be resolved or explicitly excluded before authoritative ledger posting, not silently accepted as booked FX.
5. **Separate business ledgers:** salary expense, employer contributions, employee withholding/payables and settlement differ from consultant fees, supplier GST, income-TDS payable/receivable and reimbursement. Neither a payment nor a tax deduction is automatically an expense/revenue adjustment.

Migration should follow a reviewed cutover, not an unrestricted replay of historical transactions. Zoho's [opening-balance workflow](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/settings/opening-balances.html) is a useful reference for carrying forward prior books; its [bank reconciliation](https://www.zoho.com/in/books/help/banking/reconciliation.html) requires statement/cleared totals to agree and restricts changing an already-reconciled opening balance.

- Import a mapping preview and source manifest with file hash, source IDs, row errors and counts. Choose a cutover date, base currency and approved opening trial balance; retain original exports unchanged.
- Import itemized unpaid invoices/bills, credits, advances, tax liabilities/credits, bank balances and payroll liabilities that sum to their control accounts. Do not import both a summarized opening control balance and the same subledger amounts as new income/expense.
- Use source IDs where available; flag likely duplicates by entity/party/document number/date/currency/amount for review. Same amount/date alone is not a safe deduplication key. File and row identity must make a repeated upload harmless.
- Reconcile opening trial balance, AR/AP aging, payroll/tax liabilities and bank statements; separate outstanding instruments from opening cash. Unknown mappings, missing FX and imbalances block finalization. Never plug an unexplained difference into revenue or mark the account reconciled.
- Accept the cutover only after accountant approval and an export/restore check. Keep pre-cutover history read-only; choose explicitly whether any history is informational or posted so it cannot be counted twice.

## India rules and boundaries

### Period-aware tax facts

The [Income-tax Act, 2025, as amended by Finance Act 2026](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/income_tax_act_2025_as_amended_by_fa_act_2026-pdf) took effect on 1 April 2026; the [CBDT transition release](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/press-release-income-tax-act-2025-comes-into-force-from-01-april-2026-pdf) confirms the 2026 Rules and new forms. Keep FY 2025–26 / AY 2026–27 legacy handling distinct from Tax Year 2026–27. A familiar section label is not the rule for every year.

Every implemented rule should carry official provision/notification URL, applicable period, effective dates, version/review date, eligible payer/payee/profile, inputs, rounding, coverage status and explicit exclusions. Unsupported or missing material facts block an authoritative calculation; they must not produce a zero tax result. Store the applied version with a payroll/tax snapshot. A new rule must not rewrite approved history. Boundary checks should cover the day before/on commencement, threshold minus/equal/plus one unit, regime and residence, age at the legally relevant date, missing identifiers, cumulative/year-to-date thresholds, rounding, corrections and transitions between employers. Do not add a broad rate table without the corresponding eligibility model.

### Keep the workflows distinct

| Workflow | Required distinction and acceptance |
| --- | --- |
| Employee declarations | Proof collection and manual employer review are evidence. The [2026 Rules, rule 205 / Form 124](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/notification-22-2026-1) prescribe employee evidence for salary-TDS estimation. Miru's category labels/approved amount must not bypass eligibility, shared caps or year-specific rules. |
| Payroll computation | Monthly salary TDS, prior-employer/YTD data, loss of pay, arrears, PF/ESI and state-specific obligations need named applicability and reconciliation. EPFO's [revamped ECR circular](https://www.epfindia.gov.in/site_docs/PDFs/Circulars/Y2025-2026/ECRRevamp_26092025.pdf) separates return submission from payment. An accepted output file is not proof that contributions were deposited. |
| Consultant payments | Record legal contracting entity, service/residency/location facts, invoice, fee, reimbursements and any withholding basis before net payable. Use a separately reviewed applicability decision for income-TDS/GST; a role label, Indian bank account or foreign funding label cannot decide it. Acceptance must reject both treating a US payer's India consultant as US-resident payroll and assuming that foreign payment automatically means zero Indian income tax/GST. |
| GST | GST output tax, input-credit eligibility, reverse charge, GST withholding and income-TDS are different concepts. [GSTN's GSTR-2B guidance](https://tutorial.gst.gov.in/userguide/returns/FAQ_gstr2b.htm) requires books reconciliation and prevention of duplicate credit; presence in a statement does not settle every eligibility condition. Bank reconciliation and GST reconciliation are separate. |
| Export services | A US customer alone does not establish zero-rated export. [IGST Act section 2(6), via India Code](https://www.indiacode.nic.in/bitstream/123456789/2251/1/A201713.pdf) sets supplier/recipient/place-of-supply, permitted receipt and distinct-establishment conditions. Capture the reviewed export/LUT treatment and remittance evidence; do not infer it from currency or funding account. |
| TDS certificates | Certificates are employer/deductor-issued prescribed documents, not a generated payslip. [Official Form 130 FAQ](https://www.incometaxindia.gov.in/documents/d/guest/form-130-faqs) identifies the new salary certificate and its tax-deposit reconciliation. [TRACES Form 16 guidance](https://traces61contents.tdscpc.gov.in/en/faq-dedu-form16-download.html) documents statement/PAN prerequisites for the legacy flow. Implement year-correct statements, deposits, authorized retrieval and signing before claiming official issuance. Store an imported genuine certificate separately from a preparation draft. |
| ITR preparation / filing | Uploading evidence, computing an estimate, creating valid return JSON, submission and taxpayer verification are separate states. [Income Tax ERI APIs](https://www.incometax.gov.in/iec/foportal/api-specifications) require Type-2 ERI credentials and taxpayer consent for client addition. Future filing needs approved access or an authorized provider, complete eligible data, official year-specific schemas/utility validation, acknowledgments and verification. No automatic filing/certificate integration is established here. |

The next compliance deliverable should be a reviewer-ready pack: posted register, reconciled totals, missing-data list, source evidence, versioned calculations and export files. Portal acknowledgments, challans, bank confirmations and certificates are separate attached evidence with explicit status. Do not describe preparation as filing, remittance or issuance.

## Free software, operating cost and research boundary

- Miru's checked-in [LICENSE](../../LICENSE) is MIT. Keep the core usable through self-hosting, manual CSV/document import/export, local deterministic calculations and ordinary role-based workflows without compulsory paid software, AI API calls or vendor bank feeds.
- Free software does not eliminate hosting, storage/backups, email delivery, certificates, bank/payment charges or government/intermediary onboarding costs. Show optional provider dependencies before enabling them; preserve a manual preparation/export route wherever practical.
- Documenso's [official license policy](https://github.com/documenso/documenso/blob/main/apps/docs/content/docs/policies/licenses.mdx) offers AGPL Community and commercial licensing. Calling its API alone is not described as creating a derivative work; modified network-accessible Documenso has AGPL source obligations. Keep license notices/source duties and the actual selected edition under review rather than copying its implementation into MIT Miru. Its [hosted pricing](https://documenso.com/pricing) places automation/embedded signing on paid offerings; a small hosted free allowance is not a free production integration. Self-hosted API, embedding entitlement, signing key management, email and actual completion must be proven before selecting it.
- REA's [repository/license](https://github.com/morluto/rea) permits tool evaluation; that license does not license competitor assets. Use public official feature evidence to write original requirements, tests and Rails/React code. Retain citations and minimal research metadata, not downloaded proprietary bundles in the repository. No copied UI, marketing text, private account capture or implied access to proprietary backend algorithms.

## Decision

Finish the current upload-and-sign unit, verify the existing employer document loop, then implement and reconcile one INR accounting month with a migration cutover. Reuse the employee, invoice, expense, payment and payroll-review foundations. Delay inventory/manufacturing, generalized ERP automation, broad native statutory payroll and personal-return filing until their narrower data and authorization prerequisites have been accepted.

Not checked: authenticated competitor products, production Miru, live payment/signing/tax integrations or full statutory coverage. Risk: public feature research and checked-in code are insufficient grounds to claim compliance or a deployed replacement.
