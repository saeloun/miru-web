### Beneficiary setup and validation

The create/update recipient request schemas expose ACH, domestic-wire, check, and real-time routing fields, without an international-wire routing input. Do not invent a SWIFT payload for these endpoints. [Create recipient](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/createrecipient), [update recipient](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/updaterecipient).

`POST /recipients/invites` accepts `paymentMethods: ["internationalWire"]`, contact email, `requireTaxDocument`, `sendEmail`, and either an existing `recipientId` or a new recipient name. It returns an onboarding URL and invite ID. New recipients are created when onboarding completes. Invite states include `created`, `completed`, `expired`, and `declined`; completed onboarding supplies the recipient identity. Sending an invite/email is an external action requiring its own authorization. [Invite API](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/createrecipientinvite).

The recipient read schema includes `internationalWireRoutingInfo.swiftCode`, `iban`, `countrySpecific.india.ifscCode`, address/bank details, and optional correspondent information. The field called `iban` is a provider schema name: it does not prove an Indian beneficiary needs a European-style IBAN. Validate the beneficiary's actual account details through Mercury's country-specific onboarding and receiving-bank instructions. [Recipient schema](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/getrecipient). Only supply intermediary details when instructed by the recipient. [International payments](https://support.mercury.com/hc/en-us/articles/28773219548180-Sending-international-payments).

## Approval, tracking, and uncertainty

1. Queue the request after Miru approval; retain its `requestId` and provider response. Mercury dashboard approval is required before funds are released. [Send Money](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/send-money).
2. Poll `GET /request-send-money/{requestId}`: `pendingApproval`, `approved`, `rejected`, or `cancelled`. Responses include reviewer history and may include `numberOfApproversRequired`, `requesterMayApprove`, and `scheduledSendDate`. Older requests may omit the optional fields. [Approval request](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/getsendmoneyapprovalrequest).
3. The international-wire recipe permits requester approval when org rules allow it, while the endpoint prose says the approver must differ from the token creator. Treat that as a documentation inconsistency: render recorded requirements and require the dashboard's authorization decision; never assume self-approval. [Recipe](https://docs.mercury.com/recipes/send-an-international-wire), [endpoint](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/requestsendmoney).
4. Approval requests have no webhook event today. After approval creates a transaction, use `transaction.created` / `transaction.updated` or poll. [Send Money](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/send-money). Find matching transactions with `GET /account/{accountId}/transactions?requestId=...`; transaction objects also carry `requestId`. [Transaction listing](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/listaccounttransactions).
5. Transaction states are `pending`, `sent`, `cancelled`, `failed`, `reversed`, and `blocked`. The schema exposes failure information, tracking number, estimated delivery, fee links, and related transactions. [Transaction schema](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/gettransactionbyid). Map `sent` to **Sent—Awaiting credit confirmation**, never automatically to **Credited**.
6. Public Mercury schemas reviewed do not establish final INR credited by the Indian bank, a receiving-bank conversion rate, or a FIRC/FIRA issuance API. Confirm actual INR, date, reference, and evidence separately; reconcile shortfalls. Mercury's `currencyExchangeInfo` object is not evidence of receiving-bank conversion for a USD wire. [Transaction schema](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/gettransactionbyid). Bank-issued documentation and credit confirmation are separate issue requirements.

Use an immutable idempotency key per intended beneficiary transfer, durable local state, and locking to avoid repeated submissions. The request reference calls the key a unique transaction identifier but does not document its retention window, conflict behavior, or ambiguous-timeout recovery contract. Do not assume the direct-send endpoint's documented 24-hour duplicate guard applies to international-wire approval requests. Reconcile an uncertain submission before generating a new payment identity. [Request API](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/requestsendmoney), [direct-send comparison](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/send-money).

For a confirmed failed transfer, distinguish provider-safe retry of the same request from an approved replacement transfer. For `reversed` or a return transaction, retain the original record and reconcile the returned amount; do not silently relabel it credited or re-send it. Provider failure and reversal states alone do not establish the amount recoverable. [Transaction schema](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/gettransactionbyid).

## Authentication, sandbox, and webhooks

- HTTPS API tokens support Basic or Bearer authentication. Read-only tokens cannot initiate payments or manage recipients. A Custom token can grant `RequestSendMoney` / Send Money with Approval without an IP allowlist; additional read/invite/webhook permissions must match the endpoints used. Read-write tokens require an IP allowlist. Do not assume an existing read-only CLI session grants payment capability. [Getting Started](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/getting-started).
- Custom scopes cannot currently be edited. Unused tokens are deleted after 45 days; overbroad permissions can be downgraded based on 45-day usage, with seven-day advance notices. Handle revoked/downgraded credentials without retrying a payment under another identity. [Token security policies](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/api-token-security-policies).
- Connecting customers' Mercury accounts through OAuth requires prior provider approval. Authorization Code and PKCE flows are documented; the public guide does not establish an approved Miru client or a complete refresh/scope contract. A company's own account can use API tokens instead. [OAuth integration](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/integrations-with-oauth2).
- Sandbox uses sandbox-created tokens and `https://api-sandbox.mercury.com/api/v1/`; production tokens do not work there. OAuth sandbox has its own base URL. It uses simulated balances/data and no real money. [Sandbox guide](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/using-mercury-sandbox).
- **Webhooks are unavailable in sandbox.** In production, verify `Mercury-Signature` using HMAC-SHA256 of `timestamp.raw_body`, constant-time comparison, and timestamp tolerance (provider recommends five minutes). Deduplicate event IDs and use `resourceVersion`/a refreshed resource to reconcile stale events. Acknowledge quickly: timeouts over five seconds/non-2xx may retry up to ten times over roughly one day; 4xx other than 429 are not retried. Delivery is at least once. [Webhooks](https://docs.mercury.com/reference/webhooks).

## Company policy and complete-issue boundaries

These are Saeloun requirements from [issue #2458](https://github.com/saeloun/miru-web/issues/2458), not Mercury payroll features or verified statutory guidance:

| Issue cases | Required Miru responsibility |
|---|---|
| 1: profile and bank details | Secure/masked bank information, repeated account-number confirmation, required SWIFT/IFSC as applicable, accounts verification, invoice authorization; block only affected consultants. Changes invalidate unsubmitted approval. |
| 2: configuration | Effective-dated INR salary history, funding source separate from contracting entity, schedules/holidays/PTO thresholds, preparation/approval/payment roles. |
| 3–4: monthly review and attendance | Make Payment first produces review; working days = recorded hours / 8; separate paid leave; count approved nonbillable work; prorate only approved unpaid time against schedule/service dates without double-counting holidays. Missing hours or historical negative PTO do not imply deduction. |
| 5: PTO decisions | Deduct, advance, defer, waive, or request correction with history; year-end/exit review; prevent double recovery. |
| 6: reimbursement | Reserve approved unpaid claims within cutoff; retain detail/receipts, exclude already settled/rejected/pending, carry late approval; never prorate claims with salary. |
| 7: USD wire calculation | For approved INR payable P and sourced/timestamped INR-per-USD R: buffered rate = R − 1.50; USD principal = P / buffered rate, rounded to supported currency precision. Reject nonpositive buffered rate. Refresh stale rates and renew approval. Fee is separate/configurable (currently $15 where applicable); total company cost = principal + fee. No reviewed Mercury endpoint supplies this chosen reference rate or guarantees actual INR credit. |
| 8: India funding | Approved payment instructions go to accounts with hours/days, paid leave, salary adjustments, reimbursements, total and bank details; remain awaiting payment until accounts records transfer and credit. Mercury is not involved. |
| 9: spouse split | Accounts verification/admin approval, percentage or fixed-INR allocations equal entitlement with explicit remainder; split adjusted salary, reimbursements to consultant unless approved otherwise. One principal, OUR fee, idempotency identity and lifecycle per beneficiary; only failed legs eligible for recovery; aggregate credited only when every leg is confirmed. One consultant identity/invoice. Mercury does not provide an atomic payroll split API in the reviewed contract. |
| 10: invoice | After full confirmed credit, create authorized consultant invoice/PDF or link an existing invoice; retain legal entity/tax/service/payment details, separate reimbursements/withholding/company fees; email accounts and expose to consultant; deduplicate settlement. Provider approval, processing, or sent status cannot trigger this. Tax/invoice timing remains a separate requirement. |
| 11: remittance documents | Attach receiving-bank FIRC/FIRA/advice to payment and invoice when available; track document receipt independently of credit; follow up missing documentation. A Mercury receipt is not a bank-issued FIRC. |
| 12: approval/audit | Snapshot changed inputs and renew approval; granular per-transfer states, partial credit, return/failure handling, durable deduplication, references, immutable history; tax treatment independent of funding bank. |

Mercury's client-side multi-recipient workflow sends separate sequential requests. A spouse split is therefore a Miru allocation/reconciliation workflow; neither atomic success nor regulatory acceptance of a nominated spouse beneficiary follows from the payment API. Confirm the arrangement with accounts and the receiving bank before enabling it. [Send Money](https://docs.mercury.com/docs/send-money).
