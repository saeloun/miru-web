# Interview feedback — Task 6 increment

Local implementation for [Sonam's ATS request](https://github.com/saeloun/miru-web/issues/2463). This adds feedback collection and scoped history. The later [candidate booking message increment](interview-messages-20261107.md) adds approved invitation/confirmation/reschedule/cancellation messages for recorded bookings. Task 6 remains incomplete: unbooked invitations, internal Admin notifications, feedback prompts and overdue reminders still require implementation and delivery verification. The feedback reconciliation job creates missing requests; it sends no email.

## Behavior

- A booking atomically issues one request per original panel member, retaining encrypted reviewer identity, issued questions/criteria, exact UTC booking times, candidate zone and deadline. A five-minute job backfills older bookings idempotently. Profile removal preserves submitted history and the original identity; a replacement employee cannot take over that request.
- The staff queue lists their pending/due requests in pages of 20. Submission starts at the scheduled interview time and remains available after round completion while the application is open. Late feedback is accepted and marked late. Cancelled/replaced bookings and closed applications cannot collect new feedback.
- Record the actual candidate-local date, attendance, topics, assessment, communication, concrete evidence, issued question answers and Pass/Fail/Hold/Additional Round Required. Technical skills, strengths and concerns are optional. A no-show records its date, evidence and recommendation without fabricated assessment fields. Attendance and recommendation are independent; Miru does not invent company policy or advance the stage.
- Submitted feedback is read-only. An identical accepted request recovers its original result after a lost response, including a valid uppercase UUID. Different intent conflicts. Actor/workspace/employment and issued reviewer identity are checked before recovery; four optimistic versions protect new submissions. Sensitive fields and request details are encrypted and filtered from logs.
- Admin history includes pending/submitted records across reschedules, in pages of 20. A currently assigned next-round interviewer can explicitly load prior submitted feedback, excluding cancelled bookings and Admin-only notes/pay/form data. Round completion does not restore the original candidate handover. Denied refreshes clear the private parent view as well as the feedback section.
- Drafts retain their original versions/key after conflicts or uncertain responses. Explicit discard reloads current terms. Revoked access clears the draft and candidate data. This increment sends no candidate/internal email and changes no external calendar.

## Decisions and costs

1. Keep immutable issued reviewer identity alongside a nullable live profile FK. Cost: direct unscoped SQL could corrupt that pointer; authorization checks both frozen and live identities, while the booking has a composite tenant FK.
2. Permit the original active reviewer to read minimal submitted feedback after round completion. Cost: issued criteria/time and their own feedback remain available; candidate forms/files/pay and handover stay revoked.
3. Seed new requests inside booking creation and reconcile older bookings every five minutes. Cost: older requests can appear one sweep later; new bookings are immediate.
4. Require concrete evidence and core attended assessments, with optional technical/strengths/concerns fields and all four independent recommendations. Cost: Admins must judge evidence and treat blank optional fields as unrecorded.
5. Bound the request envelope at 1 MiB so twenty allowed 5,000-character Unicode answers and rubric fields fit. Cost: a submission can consume up to that bound; all per-field limits remain.
